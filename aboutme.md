    <p class="section-kicker">What to expect</p>
    <h2>A practical bias toward useful, inspectable systems.</h2>
    <p>The pattern is straightforward. I try to make the problem easier to see, the logic easier to follow, and the handoff easier for the next team.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="quiz-grid">
    <article class="quiz-card">
      <p class="mini-label">Start</p>
      <h3>Fix the definition before the presentation layer.</h3>
      <p>If people do not trust the source or the logic, a cleaner dashboard will not solve the real problem.</p>
    </article>

    <article class="quiz-card">
      <p class="mini-label">Build</p>
      <h3>Make the workflow easier for the next team to run.</h3>
      <p>The goal is not just to ship something useful once. It is to leave behind something that keeps working without constant repair.</p>
    </article>

    <article class="quiz-card">
      <p class="mini-label">Use</p>
      <h3>Keep the decision context visible.</h3>
      <p>I care most about work that helps someone make a better call, not just work that produces an interesting output.</p>
    </article>
  </div>
