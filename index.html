How I Work

The dashboard is rarely the first problem.

I usually start where trust has broken: mismatched customer records, unclear metric definitions, manual review queues, or pipelines people rely on but cannot inspect. The design goal is a calmer operating surface, not a busier one.

Useful pattern

Find the drift Identify where records, definitions, or ownership stopped matching the workflow.

Make the logic visible Put tests, lineage, and assumptions where another person can inspect them.

Leave a better handoff Ship something that keeps working after the first analysis or dashboard review.
